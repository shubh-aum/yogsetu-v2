Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

What we collect

Account details you provide (name, email, phone), profile content you add (bio, certifications, photos), and usage data needed to operate connection requests and the requirement board.

How we use it

To operate the verification process, match connection requests, send email notifications, and maintain the audit trail described in our Terms. We do not sell personal data to third parties.

Contact details & connections

A client's or teacher's contact details are shared with the other party only once a connection request is approved — never before.

Your choices

You can request a copy of your data or ask us to delete your account at any time by contacting hello@yogsetu.com.
