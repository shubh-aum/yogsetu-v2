01 · Choosing a teacher
There are so many profiles — how do I know who's right for me?
YS

Start with the style strip or the search bar above — filter by city, style and mode first. Then sort by rating or experience, and check each teacher's specialization line and student reviews before reaching out.

02 · Verified badge
What does the "Verified" badge actually mean?
YS

It means we checked that teacher's certification directly with the issuing body — Yoga Alliance, AYUSH, YTTAI or an equivalent — plus an ID check, before their profile ever went live. Unverified profiles never appear in search.

03 · Pricing
Why does the price per session vary so much between teachers?
YS

Experience, city, teaching mode and specialization (like prenatal or therapeutic yoga) all factor in. Use the price filter in the sidebar, or sort by "Price: low to high" to match your budget.

04 · Request info
What happens after I click "Request info"?
YS

The teacher gets notified and can approve or decline your request. Once approved, contact details are shared so you can set up a trial directly — your first connection on YogSetu is free, no platform fee.
