Using YogSetu

YogSetu is a connection-request platform for yoga teachers and clients. We verify teacher credentials with issuing bodies but do not employ teachers or guarantee outcomes of any session.

Rate limits

To prevent spam, clients are limited to 5 connection requests and 5 requirement postings per day.

Verification

Teacher verification badges are reviewed annually. YogSetu may suspend a listing that no longer meets verification standards.

Off-platform payments

Session pricing and payment are agreed directly between client and teacher after a connection is approved. YogSetu is not a party to that transaction.
