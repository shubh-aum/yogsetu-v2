About YogSetu

About
Us

A YogKulam initiative — proof, not profiles.

Most people find a yoga teacher through a random agency, a contact's recommendation, or an Instagram reel — with no way to check whether the training claimed is real. YogSetu exists to fix that one problem: proof, before you commit to a teacher.

Meet the founder

Built by someone who teaches, not just manages.

YogKulam has trained and certified yoga teachers for over 12 years — 40,000+ teachers, 60,000+ students. YogSetu was built to put that same verification discipline behind every listing: no credential goes live until it's been checked with the body that actually issued it.
