06 The standard

What verified actually means here

Most directories take a teacher's word for it. We check the paperwork with the people who issued it.

  1. Documents, not claims

    Every teacher uploads their training certificate and accreditation papers from the college that issued them.

    Awaiting documentStamped ✓

  2. Checked with the issuer

    Our team confirms each credential directly against the recognised body that awarded it. No self-declared training.

    Awaiting confirmationStamped ✓

  3. Re-checked every year

    The badge expires annually. A teacher stays listed only while their certification is current.

    Awaiting renewalStamped ✓
