05Questions

Ask before you connect

Questions here are visible to other visitors too — so you're not the only one who gets the answer.

Do you bring props, or do we need our own mat and blocks?
AG

I provide mats, blocks and a bolster for in-studio sessions — you don't need to bring anything. For online sessions, a mat and one or two firm cushions work fine.

Can I switch between online and in-studio week to week?
AG

Yes — the 8-session pack works across both formats, so you can mix based on your week. Just mention your preference for each session when you book.
